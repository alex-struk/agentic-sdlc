# 0002 — Constitution sources for P1–P3

**Status:** accepted · 2026-09-06

**Decision.** The platform articles P1 (accessibility), P2 (design system) and
P3 (privacy) cite a source only where a fetched government page states the
rule the article asserts. Where a page does not, the article cites
`Source: convention` and carries a note that a policy citation is still to be
found, rather than a link that does not support the claim.

**What was checked.**

- **P1 — Accessibility.** `https://digital.gov.bc.ca/design/wcag/intro/` states
  that the Province has "been committed to achieving Web Content Accessibility
  Guidelines (WCAG), Level AA compliance for websites and applications" since
  2014, and separately states that the current version is WCAG 2.2, with 2.2
  conformance subsuming 2.1. The page does not state a requirement pinned to
  WCAG 2.1 specifically, which is the version P1 asserts. Source changed to
  `convention`.
- **P2 — Design system.** `https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/digital/design-system`
  describes the B.C. Design System as "an open source project, to help
  software developers and designers in the B.C. government deliver a
  consistent digital user experience for the public." It does not state that
  the design system is the provincial standard for digital services. Source
  changed to `convention`.
- **P3 — Privacy.** `https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/services-for-government/information-management-technology/privacy/privacy-impact-assessments`
  states that "Section 69 (5) ... of the Freedom of Information and Protection
  of Privacy Act (FOIPPA) requires you to conduct a PIA" and that a PIA is
  needed "to determine whether your project involves personal information and
  if so, how you'll protect the information you collect or use." This
  supports the article's requirement that a PIA precede collection of
  personal information. Source kept as cited.

**Consequences.** P1 and P2 read `Source: convention` with a one-line note
that the policy citation is still to be found, so the constitution does not
carry a link that overstates what it supports. P3 keeps its citation. A future
task can replace the P1 and P2 notes once a page stating those specific rules
is located.
